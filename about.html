A little about our kitchen

Made with care, shared with love

Sweet Crumb is a small home bakery in Gulberg, where every bake starts with a warm oven and a little patience.

We believe the best treats feel like they belong at your table. A cake for a family celebration, cookies alongside a cup of tea, or a fresh loaf for breakfast — we make each one in small batches, right here in Lahore.

Our menu changes with what we love to bake. Tell us what you’re celebrating or what you’re craving, and we’ll be happy to help you choose.
